# Tokenomics & Governance (Post-Revenue)

## The Token: $NXS
-   **Utility**: Staking for node operators (security bond), Governance voting, Payment currency (optional, can use stablecoins).
-   **Supply**: Fixed supply. Deflationary (buyback and burn from protocol fees).

## Distribution
-   **Community/Miners**: 60% (Earned by providing storage/compute).
-   **Core Team**: 20% (Vested over 4 years).
-   **Investors**: 0% (We are bootstrapping).
-   **Treasury**: 20% (For grants and ecosystem growth).

## Governance
-   **Phase 1**: Dictatorship (Core Team decides). Speed is key.
-   **Phase 2**: Consultative (Community signals, Team decides).
-   **Phase 3**: DAO (Token holders vote on protocol upgrades).
